#rom debug
#save after-first-dungeon
# DX rule checks through a debug probe (gDxTestProbe: the next turn stores what a few rules work out):
# Tight Formation (Tiny Woods 1F, seed 99991: the partner starts next to the leader) and the
# post-game fallback recruit rate for a story boss Red never lets join (Gengar)
mash START sMainMenu 4 != 0 3000
write gDxTestSeed 4 99991
write gDxForcePostgame 1 1
wait 60
press UP
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
press A
mash A gDungeon 4 != 0 1800
wait 600
expect **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon+@gDxOffEntityPos 2 == 19
expect **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityPos 2 == 18
# leader (recruit slot 42) holds Tight Formation (32)
write *gRecruitedPokemonRef+3733 1 32
write gDxTestProbe 1 1
hold A+B 60
wait 30
expect gDxTestProbe 1 == 0
# Earthquake (hits the whole room): 10% less with one teammate adjacent; Tackle (one tile): unchanged
expect gDxTestOut 2 == 90
expect gDxTestOut+2 2 == 100
# Gengar (-999 in Red) gets the 10% fallback after the credits; an ordinary foe keeps -999
expect gDxTestOut+4 2 == 100
expect gDxTestOut+6 2 == -999
