# (the partner too), with a "grew to II!" message; the Moves menu shows the level and a bar (Tackle) or a
# silver star (Growl, a status move, doesn't grow)

# the longest name at a long level: Dazzling Gleam (0x19B) at III (30 uses), to see it fits its row (no PP:
# A still picks Tackle)

write ***gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityInfo+@gDxOffInfoMoves!5 1 0
write ***gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon+@gDxOffEntityInfo+@gDxOffInfoTypes 1 0

press A
wait 100
shot 2_grew
wait 150
