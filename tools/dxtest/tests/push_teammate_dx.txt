#rom debug
#save after-first-dungeon
# DX push: the leader swaps places with a teammate even while it is asleep (Red refuses)
mash START sMainMenu 4 != 0 3000
write gDxTestSeed 4 99991
wait 60
press UP
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
press A
wait 30
press A
mash A gDungeon 4 != 0 1800
wait 600
shot 1_in_dungeon
read lx0 **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon+@gDxOffEntityPos 2
read px0 **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityPos 2
read py0 **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityPos!2 2
# with this seed the partner starts just left of the leader; put it to sleep for 50 turns
read slx **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon+@gDxOffEntityPos 2
read sly **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon+@gDxOffEntityPos!2 2
read spx **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityPos 2
read spy **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityPos!2 2
calc sleft slx - 1
cmpreads spx == sleft
cmpreads spy == sly
write ***gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityInfo+@gDxOffInfoSleep 1 1
write ***gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityInfo+@gDxOffInfoSleep!1 1 50
wait 30
shot 2_partner_asleep
press LEFT
wait 90
shot 3_pushed_past
# they swapped places
read alx **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon+@gDxOffEntityPos 2
read apx **gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityPos 2
cmpreads alx == spx
cmpreads apx == slx
expect ***gDungeon+@gDxOffTeamPokemon!4+@gDxOffEntityInfo+@gDxOffInfoSleep 1 == 1
